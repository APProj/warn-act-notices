Oregon WARN notices — layoff notice list, updated daily

All WARN Act layoff notices published by Oregon, normalized into one clean schema (employer, location, workers affected, notice date, layoff date) and refreshed every day. Updated 2026-09-03. The free files carry no delay — every notice is published the day the pipeline sees it. Source last scraped: 2026-09-02 18:41 UTC · newest notice on file: 2026-09-01. Oregon's list sometimes gives only the facility in its company column (e.g. 'Portland Facility', '#4184 - Portland') and lists a phased notice once per layoff date. We keep those rows exactly as the state published them; the home-page 'biggest notices' table skips rows that name no employer and groups phases per notice.
