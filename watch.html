How it works

  1. You pay once and type the employers you want watched (comma separated), plus any two-letter state codes, right at checkout.
  2. We check them daily. Our pipeline scrapes 39 state labor-department portals, normalizes every notice into one schema, and matches your terms against the same-day build — on the employer name as filed, on our canonical employer name and on the trade name, so "general motors" also catches "General Motors, LLC -- Lansing Region" and store-number variants.
  3. Matches land on your page and feed. An unlisted alert page (address derived locally in your browser from your purchase email and a passphrase you choose at checkout — we never publish the email, and the email alone does not open it) plus an RSS feed you can point any reader, Slack or Teams channel at. Find yours any time at https://apventureengine.github.io/warn-act-notices/alerts/.

Honest limits — please read before paying

  • No email yet. Alerts are a page plus an RSS feed. Email is in progress and buyers get it at no extra cost when it ships. If you need email today, Layoff Lookout (17 states) sends it and starts at $2/mo.
  • 39 states, not 50. The states we do not cover are listed on the dataset page; a state is added the day it publishes a usable public listing.
  • We are as fast as the state agency. Some portals publish same-day; some post in weekly batches. We alert the day our scrape sees a notice — we cannot beat the source.
  • No archive spam. When a state republishes its back catalogue, thousands of old notices arrive at once. A notice whose own date is more than 120 days older than the day we first saw it is treated as history, not news, and never paged to you.
  • Matching is token-based, so subsidiaries and re-notices are caught but the occasional near-miss happens. Every alert links to the employer's page so you can check.
