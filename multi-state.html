FAQ

Why can't I get this from a state WARN feed?

Because each state publishes only its own notices, under whatever name the employer used on that form. Seeing that one company is cutting in six states means normalizing 39 portals into one schema and resolving employer names across them. That is the whole job.

How are employer names matched across states?

Deterministic canonicalization: legal suffixes (Inc, LLC, Corp) are stripped, 'dba'/'aka' aliases split off, site and store tails removed, and ALL-CAPS names title-cased with an acronym list. No fuzzy guessing — two names merge only if they reduce to the same string, so this page undercounts rather than inventing matches.

How current is it?

Rebuilt every day by the same pipeline that publishes the free dataset. This build is dated 2026-09-05.
