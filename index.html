Is the paid tier worth it? An honest answer.

The free files are the whole dataset from 2024 on — no delay, no login — and they stay free. None of it is secret: the state portals are public and biglocalnews/warn-scraper is open source. We measured the gap on 2026-09-03, when the archive held 45,755 rows: only 1,188 of them (2.6%) were older than what the state portals still serve today. So the $199 archive is not scarce data — it is 39 portals already scraped, de-duplicated into one schema with stable ids, as a 3.7 MB ZIP (CSV + JSON) you can load in one line.

What you are paying for is maintenance. Portals change layout, drop fields and go quiet; keeping 39 of them normalized every day is the work. WARN Watch is the same trade: a year of daily checks against your employer list that you never have to keep running. If that is not worth more to you than an afternoon and a cron job, use the free files with our blessing — that is what they are for.

Paid tiers, refunds, contact

There are exactly two paid products, and neither of them is access to this data. The daily dataset above — every state we cover, 2024 to today, no delay, CSV + JSON + RSS — is free and stays free. We sell (1) WARN Watch, which runs your employer and state list against every refresh and tells you the day a notice lands, and (2) the 1988–present historical archive, which is the back catalogue the free files do not include. There is no subscription and nothing auto-renews; an earlier "real-time feed" SKU was retired when we dropped the free tier's delay to zero, which is why you will not find it for sale anywhere on this page.

What the two paid tiers are for. WARN Watch ($49/year, one payment) takes up to 25 employer terms and any number of whole-state watches, matches them against every daily refresh for 365 days, and publishes hits to a private alert page + RSS feed — no login, no email required; limits and a live comparison are on the WARN Watch page. The historical archive ($199 one-time) is every notice we hold back to 1988 — 45,760 rows across all covered states versus the 9,428 rows (2024–present) in the free files. It arrives in two parts, and we would rather you know before you pay than after: (1) the ZIP that downloads instantly from Gumroad is the last monthly build we uploaded there (the listing states its exact row and state count); (2) the complete current build — CSV + JSON + coverage metadata, rebuilt every month and yours to keep — comes from the private GitHub repo you are invited to after checkout, usually within a few hours and at most 24h. Enter your GitHub username at checkout; on the repo, the green "Code" button offers "Download ZIP", so no git is required. Buy the archive if you need the back-catalogue once and keep it forever; buy WARN Watch if you want your own employers and states checked for you every day. Try before you buy: one state's complete back-catalogue is free as a sample — Oregon, 1988→present, 1,369 rows, identical schema to the archive.
