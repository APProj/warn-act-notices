Is the paid tier worth it? An honest answer.

The free files are the whole dataset from 2024 on — no delay, no login — and they stay free. None of it is secret: the state portals are public and biglocalnews/warn-scraper is open source. We measured the gap on 2026-09-03, when the archive held 45,755 rows: only 1,188 of them (2.6%) were older than what the state portals still serve today. So the $199 archive is not scarce data — it is 39 portals already scraped, de-duplicated into one schema with stable ids, as a 7 MB CSV (plus JSON) you can load in one line.

What you are paying for is maintenance. Portals change layout, drop fields and go quiet; keeping 39 of them normalized every day is the work. WARN Watch is the same trade: a year of daily checks against your employer list that you never have to keep running. If that is not worth more to you than an afternoon and a cron job, use the free files with our blessing — that is what they are for.

Paid tiers, refunds, contact

There are exactly two paid products, and neither of them is access to this data. The daily dataset above — every state we cover, 2024 to today, no delay, CSV + JSON + RSS — is free and stays free. We sell (1) WARN Watch, which runs your employer and state list against every refresh and tells you the day a notice lands, and (2) the 1988–present historical archive, which is the back catalogue the free files do not include. There is no subscription and nothing auto-renews; a third product that used to sell same-day access was withdrawn once the free files stopped being held back, which is why you will not find it for sale anywhere on this page.

What the two paid tiers are for. WARN Watch ($49/year, one payment) takes up to 25 employer terms and any number of whole-state watches, matches them against every daily refresh for 365 days, and publishes hits to a private alert page + RSS feed — no login, no email required; limits and a live comparison are on the WARN Watch page. The historical archive ($199 one-time) is every notice we hold back to 1988 — 45,777 rows across all covered states versus the 9,445 rows (2024–present) in the free files. How it is delivered, so you know before you pay rather than after: the build is a ZIP attached to the Gumroad listing, so it downloads from your receipt and library the moment you pay — no account here, no GitHub, no email wait. Inside: CSV + JSON + coverage metadata + README, about 3.9 MB. When the pipeline publishes a newer build we replace that file, and Gumroad always serves the current one, so re-downloading later gets you fresher data at no extra cost. Optionally, enter a GitHub username at checkout and an automated job also invites you to a private repo mirror (rebuilt daily) if you would rather git pull than re-download; that box really is optional and leaving it blank costs you nothing. Buy the archive if you need the back-catalogue once and keep it forever; buy WARN Watch if you want your own employers and states checked for you every day.
