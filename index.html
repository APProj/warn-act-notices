Paid tiers, refunds, contact

What the two paid tiers are for. The real-time feed ($49/mo) is a private GitHub repo that receives every new notice the moment our pipeline sees it — no 48h delay — and includes the rolling history while you subscribe; access ends when the subscription ends. The historical archive ($199 one-time) is a permanent snapshot download (ZIP: CSV + JSON + coverage metadata) of every notice we hold back to 1988 — 44,906 rows across all covered states versus the 7,099 rows (2024–present) in the free files. Buy the archive if you need the back-catalogue once and keep it forever; subscribe if you need today's notices today.

Delivery. Both tiers ask for your GitHub username at checkout. An automated job sends the repo invitation within one refresh cycle (usually a few hours, at most 24h).

Refunds. Archive: full refund within 7 days of purchase if the data is not as described — just ask. Real-time feed: cancel any time from your Gumroad receipt or library; you keep access until the end of the paid month, and if the invite never arrives we refund the month in full.

Contact. Open an issue at github.com/APVentureEngine/warn-act-notices/issues (templates for data errors, missing invites and general questions) — it is checked on every refresh cycle, daily or better. Gumroad buyers can also reply to their receipt email, which reaches us through Gumroad.

Sources: official state labor department WARN listings, collected via Apache-2.0 biglocalnews/warn-scraper plus our normalization layer. Dataset CC BY 4.0. Best-effort public records — verify critical rows against the state source. Not affiliated with any state agency. GitHub · contact
